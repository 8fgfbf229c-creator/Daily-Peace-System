# Using Reset

Reset is meant to be opened for a few minutes, on a phone, whenever you want to come back to yourself. Nothing is counted against you if you skip a day.

## First visit

Read the welcome, then confirm that you understand Reset is not a substitute for professional care. You can add a first name if you want the greeting to use it. That name stays on this device.

## A daily reset

On Home, open **5-Step Calm Flow**. Tell Reset how you feel. Overwhelmed, stressed, and tired lead into the five steps: breathe, release, ground, let one thing go, and read today’s affirmation. Calm opens today’s gratitude instead.

You can also open **Gratitude** or **Night Reset** directly.

## Night Reset

Open it from the Night Reset card or the Night tab. Five short steps help you set the day down, soften your body, breathe in a 4-7-8 pattern, name one gratitude, and give yourself permission to rest. Continue when you are ready, or let a step move on by itself. Close leaves without marking it complete.

## Meditations

Meditate has six sessions: Morning Awakening (5 min), Anxiety Relief (4 min), Inner Stillness (5 min), Heart of Gratitude (5 min), Evening Wind-Down (6 min), and Deep Sleep Journey (8 min). The time on the card is the time the session runs.

Begin when you are ready. Pause holds the words. Next moves ahead. Close stops the sound, including any Home ambience that was playing. Each session has its own generated sound: morning air, rain, a singing bowl, warm tones, ocean, or deep calm tones. You can turn session sound off in More.

## Sound on Home

Rain, Ocean, and Forest are generated in the browser. Stop sound ends them. Starting a meditation also stops them, so they do not keep playing afterward.

## Journal

Write under today’s date. Entries save on this device as you type. Past entries opens earlier days. Export journal downloads a Markdown file. Clear asks before it removes today’s writing. Nothing is uploaded.

## Learn, history, and settings

More holds Learn, the quiet record, privacy, appearance, sound, and reminders.

The quiet record lists resets, meditations, gratitude, and journal moments. It does not show a streak.

Appearance can follow the time of day (dark from 8pm to 6am), or stay light or dark.

Reminders show a note inside Reset around the morning and evening times you choose. If you allow notifications, one can also appear while Reset is open. A browser cannot promise an alert after the app is fully closed, because Reset has no server to send one.

## If you need a person

**I need immediate help** is on Home and on the check-in screen. It lists:

- Emergency numbers: 911 (US & Canada), 112 (EU, including Germany), 999 (UK)
- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US), call or text
- Telefonseelsorge (Germany): 0800 111 0 111 and 0800 111 0 222
- Samaritans (UK & Ireland): 116 123
- [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) for a line in another country

Tapping a number opens your phone dialer. Reset cannot place the call for you.

## Install and offline

Add Reset to your home screen so it opens like an app.

- iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen
- Android: browser menu, then Install app or Add to Home Screen

The first open needs a connection. After that, the installed copy works offline, including the photographs, fonts, journal, and generated sound.

## If something looks wrong

Your writing is in this browser’s site data. Export the journal before you clear site data. Clearing site data removes the journal and history from that device.
