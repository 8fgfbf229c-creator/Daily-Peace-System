# Privacy

Reset keeps your information on the device you are using. There is no account and no Reset server.

## What stays on the device

The browser stores, in `localStorage` for this site:

- Your journal
- Your name, if you add one
- Appearance, sound, and reminder settings
- A local list of resets, meditations, gratitude moments, and journal days
- Whether you have accepted the care notice

That information is not sent to Reset, and Reset does not include analytics.

## What leaves the device

- **Export journal** downloads a file to your device. It is not uploaded.
- **Helpline links** open your phone dialer or the helpline’s own website. Reset does not see those calls or visits.
- **Install and offline** use a service worker that caches the app’s own files (pages, fonts, and photographs) on your device. It does not upload your journal.

## Deleting it

Clear this site’s data in the browser, or remove the installed home-screen app and its website data. That deletes the journal, history, and settings stored there. Export first if you want a copy.

## Care

Reset is not a substitute for professional care. It is a self-guided calm practice. If you are in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, use Immediate Help and contact local emergency services or a crisis line.
